2. Tables

Every table also has id (BigAutoField primary key). Times are stored as timezone-aware UTC and shown in Asia/Kolkata (KAN-18). FK = foreign key. "PROTECT" = cannot delete the parent while children exist (keeps history).

Accounts are created/invited by the coordinator. An ORG_REP is also the mentor role (proposal section 11).
